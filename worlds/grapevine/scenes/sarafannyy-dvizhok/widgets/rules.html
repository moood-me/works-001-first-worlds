The Grapevine Engine

Morning. By evening at least five people in the yard must be sure of it: 🛡️ the pond is protected by the state. The pond is in a mood today, so don't whisper to it.

Pick a phrase, click a resident and whisper it to them. Each retells it to the neighbours in their own way: the cat makes it all about food, Granny Zina about her TV series, the pigeon about a conspiracy, the street lamp about restricted sites, Petka about heroes, Lyuba passes it on word for word, and the pond takes everything personally.
