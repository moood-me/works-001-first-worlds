# works-001: Japanese (2026-10-29)

Павел: ещё язык — японский.

## What

- All 46 worlds under `worlds/` declare `"languages": ["en", "ru", "ja"]`; `check --write` laid out every `ja` file
  (the world's `lang/ja.json`, each scene's `scenes/<folder>/lang/ja.json`) and all 860 texts are translated from the
  English, with the Russian as a second view.
- The archived games in `non-bespoke-games-archive/` (`_game.json`, not taken for games) are left as they are.
- Narration literary and calm; interface short and polite; signs and labels short. The logbook's entries keep each
  writer's voice (the keeper's terse log, a child's hiragana, a technician's report); its four scenes share one wording.

## Names chosen

- Russian names in the usual katakana: ヴェーラ (Vera), ジーナばあさん (Granny Zina), ペーチカ, リューバ, セリョーガ, セールイ (Sery),
  ヴィチョーク, レーナ, ゲーナ, シヌイリ (Shnyr), シャーリク, ニーナおばあちゃん (things-are-honest), ゴルデーエフ, ストロゴワ医師;
  in the logbook サヴェーリイ・アンドレーイチ, エフィームィチ, ニーナ, ミーチャ (Dmitry — ドミトリー), リョーヴァ, ジューコフ.
- Ships in 〈〉: 〈ラドガ〉 (Ladoga), 〈クレチェト〉 (Krechet); the ship's computer LIDA — リダ.
- The lighthouse cat Wick — シン (from ランプの芯, said once where he is named).
- Japanese characters as they are: ヒカリ, ケンタ, 先輩, 北南高校 (Kita-Minami), ハルナ, ソウタ; Tsoi — ツォイ, KINO — キノー.
