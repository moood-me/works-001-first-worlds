# works-001: German (2026-10-29)

Why: «Павел: ещё язык — немецкий». moood's `check` knows `de` on its branch `de`; this repository got German on its
own branch `de`.

## What and how

- **The worlds.** All 46 worlds: `"de"` added to `"languages"` (after `"ru"`); `check --write` laid out the `de` files
  beside the `ru` ones (each scene's `lang/de.json`, each world's `lang/de.json`); every entry translated — 860 texts in
  118 scopes. Translated from the English source, the Russian read beside it for meaning and tone. `check --write` also
  rewrote the `where` of the existing `ru` files to paths from the repository's root (its present layout); their
  translations are unchanged.
- **The archived games** (`non-bespoke-games-archive/`, `_game.json`) are not games for `check` and were left as they are.
- **Checked:** `check` on the whole repository — "all right — 860 texts in 118 scopes".

## Choices

- The player is «du» everywhere, also on the signs of `window-3` («DEINE NUMMER») and in the evil therapist's office
  (the Russian has «вы» there).
- Signs drawn in the scenes are translated to fit their places: `TELEFON`, `HOTEL` (five letters, drawn one by one),
  `HIMMEL` on the hopscotch, `ZOI LEBT`, `KRYO-3`, `SCHLEUSE 2: {state}` with its states (`VERRIEGELT`, `VERKLEMMT`,
  `ANGELEHNT`, `OFFEN`).
- Russian names in German transliteration: Strogowa, Serjoga, Seryj, Witjok, Ljuba, Oma Sina, Scharik, Schnyr,
  Gordejew, Jefimytsch, Dmitri/Mitja, Ljowa, Schukow, Saweli Andrejitsch; the ship «Kretschet» (feminine, as ships
  are); the cat Фитиль/Wick is «Docht». Death in `death-in-the-park` is «der Tod» (masculine in German).
- In `logbook` the wind directions are German (`O` for east, `NO` for north-east); the logbook itself is «Wachbuch».
